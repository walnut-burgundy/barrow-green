## Frontier rules

- “Located” is not “spidered.”
- A paywall is recorded as a frontier, not treated as evidence about unseen content.
- Search snippets can locate a source but do not substitute for inspecting its bibliography.
- No private addresses or non-scholarly personal details are propagated.
